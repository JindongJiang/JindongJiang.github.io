About

I am a Research Scientist at NVIDIA Research. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from Rutgers University in April 2025, advised by Prof. Sungjin Ahn.

My research interests lie in agentic AI and the development of efficient large language and vision-language models. I have also worked on computer vision, structured representation learning, and world models.

News

  • May 2026: We released Nemotron-Labs-Diffusion, a tri-mode LM unifying autoregressive, diffusion, and self-speculation decoding in a single architecture.
  • Apr 2026: We released Recursive Multi-Agent Systems, a framework for recursive agent collaboration in latent space.
  • Mar 2026: We released Stateful Token Reduction for hybrid VLMs: 75% fewer tokens and 4.2× faster prefilling with the same or better long-video accuracy.
  • Feb 2026: Our 4DP-QA, a geometry-grounded VLM training recipe for 4D spatiotemporal understanding, was accepted to CVPR 2026.
  • Sep 2025: Our Eagle 2.5, an 8B VLM that matches GPT-4o and models 10× larger on long-video understanding, was accepted to NeurIPS 2025.
  • May 2025: I joined Learning and Perception Research Group as a Research Scientist.
  • Apr 2025: I successfully completed my Ph.D. defense at Rutgers University.
